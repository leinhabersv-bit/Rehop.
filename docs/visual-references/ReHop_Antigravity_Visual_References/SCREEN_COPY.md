|---|---|---|---|
| Landing | Your ride. No interruptions. | Find a ride | The intelligent ride companion that finds, monitors and recovers your pickup. |

| Comparison | Available rides. | Continue with Provider A | Prices and ETAs shown are simulated. |
| Confirmation | Ride confirmed. | Track your ride | ReHop will keep an eye on pickup progress. |

Provider A/B/C/D are illustrative providers; the sample amounts, ETAs, booking IDs and locations are not live.
